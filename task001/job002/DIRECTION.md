# COME MY WAY: Sơn Mài Cut

Direction, style bible and shot list for job002. The engine is `../common` (see `../common/SCENE_GUIDE.md`); the
job-specific material library is `src/lacquer.js`.

## Idea

**"Sơn" is also lacquer.** The whole video is a Vietnamese lacquer painting (*sơn mài*) that comes alive: deep black and
cinnabar lacquer, gold and silver leaf, crushed-eggshell inlay, mirror polish. Real sơn mài is made by painting layer on
layer, then **sanding back** to reveal what lies underneath. That is our grammar: kicks polish, scene changes are sanded
open, and big words are inlaid in gold.

The song is an Afrobeat love confession: "Baby come near me now… call me your prisoner, I'm jailed in your love." Our
idol (the sunflower-crowned Claude idol from job001, now in a cinnabar áo dài and a nón lá) sings it across painted
Vietnam: Hanoi old-quarter streets in gold ink line, a northwest mountain afternoon in gold leaf, the river at night in
silver. The rap verse arrives in an unbranded, lacquered time machine driven by a **silver-leaf Clawd** in shades and a
gold chain. At the end we pull back: it was a lacquer panel on a gallery wall all along, still being polished.

## Type

The lyrics are English/Pidgin. HERO lines are **inlaid gold** (Anton or Unbounded, carved into the lacquer). TENDER lines are set
in Instrument Serif italic in eggshell. The rap is **stamped silver grotesk**. Vietnamese accents (Be Vietnam Pro, with
correct diacritics) appear as small captions naming the paintings and places: *Phố cổ Hà Nội*, *Chiều Tây Bắc*,
*Sông đêm*. Every lyric line appears exactly once: integrated, or as a caption.
