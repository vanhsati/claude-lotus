# COME MY WAY: Sơn Mài Cut (draft direction)

Draft written before the audio arrived. Timings, the section map and the shot list are filled in once
`source/` has the track and `common/tools/analyze_audio.py` has produced the beat grid.

## Idea

**"Sơn" is also lacquer.** The whole video is a Vietnamese lacquer painting (*sơn mài*) that comes alive: deep black and
cinnabar-red lacquer, gold and silver leaf, crushed eggshell inlay, and a mirror polish. The official MV quotes classic
Vietnamese paintings and a DeLorean. This version turns that into its own world: a lacquer panel that is sanded back layer
by layer to the beat, revealing what is underneath. That mirrors how real sơn mài is made (paint, dry, sand, reveal).

The song is an Afrobeat / hip-hop love confession ("come near me now", "call me your prisoner, I'm jailed in your love"). The
visual story is a chase and a pull between two paper-and-lacquer characters across painted Vietnam: Hanoi old-quarter
streets drawn in ink lines, a northwest mountain afternoon in gold leaf, the river at night in silver. A DeLorean-like
time machine (unbranded, lacquered) carries them between paintings.

## Why it's fun and what JS does well here

* **Sanding reveals** as transitions and beat hits: a top lacquer layer is rubbed away in brush-shaped strokes (canvas masks)
  to show gold or the next scene underneath. Every kick polishes a patch.
* **Gold-leaf flakes** that catch light as the camera moves (a specular sweep across the gold).
* **Eggshell inlay**: a Voronoi crackle mosaic that assembles into shapes and text.
* **Ink-line streets** in the manner of Hanoi street paintings, drawn on stroke by stroke.
* **Afrobeat groove**: the choreography loops on the log-drum pattern, with body-roll and shoulder-bounce moves in the dance rig.

## Palette (lacquer, not riso)

| silver leaf `#C9CCD1` | night scenes |
| eggshell `#F3EBDD` | inlay mosaic |
| deep brown `#4A2A18` | mid layers |

## Type

The lyrics are bilingual. Vietnamese lines are set in a display serif carved into the gold. The English rap verse is set
as bold stamped grotesk that the lacquer "cuts" through. Big type opens the video and lands on the hook, and verse lines
run as smaller captions.

## Cast (original designs, no likenesses)

* **The Lover**: a lacquer paper cut-out in a white áo dài-inspired jacket with gold-leaf embroidery.
* **The Visitor** (the rap verse): a figure made of silver leaf, arriving in the time machine.
* Backup dancers: gold-leaf silhouettes that tile like a lacquer screen.

No drawings of real people. The song's artists are credited only in text.
