# KHUÔN MẶT ĐÁNG THƯƠNG: Lụa & Neon

Direction for job003 (Sơn Tùng M-TP, 2015). The engine is `../common`, and the job's material library is `src/silk.js`.

## Idea

A breakup song in two textures that fight each other:

* **Lụa** (silk painting, *tranh lụa*): the memory. Soft watercolour washes on raw silk, ink lines, muted indigo, rose
  and celadon. Colours bleed like tears. Verses live here.
* **Neon**: the present. Hard neon tube lines on a wet black night street, 2015 electro-pop energy. Choruses and the
  final drop live here.

At every chorus the silk **tears** or **dissolves** into neon; at every verse the neon **drips** back into watercolour.
The recurring image is a **face**: the sunflower-crowned idol drawn as a silk-painting portrait, whose painted tears
bleed down the silk. The "pitiful face" is also a porcelain mask that cracks. **Cánh hoa úa tàn** (withered petals)
is the other motif: her own petal crown and a silk-painted flower lose petals that drift, curl and dissolve into water.

No real people are drawn. The idol is the Claude idol from job001 and job002 in a new medium.

## Type

Vietnamese lines are brushed in the silk sections (a calligraphic serif in ink that bleeds slightly, FONT.vnI). In the neon
sections they are built as neon tube lettering (a rounded sans with a glow). The hook "KHUÔN MẶT ĐÁNG THƯƠNG" is the one
big recurring title: brushed on silk the first time, neon the second, and both at once (neon over silk) in the final drop.

## Shot sketch

1. **Silence / intro (0–25.6):** a blank silk scroll unrolls. An ink brush paints the idol's face in a few strokes; a
   single tear blooms and runs down the silk. The title is brushed in, then the city lights of the neon world bleed through
   the back of the silk.
2. **Beat in (25.6–33.2):** the silk is backlit; the neon street shows through as blurred colour, pulsing on the kick.
3. **Verse 1 (33.2–61):** silk memories of the two walking at night: a watercolour Hanoi lakeside, lamplight in ochre washes,
   rain as ink dots, a shared umbrella. Lyrics brushed in the margins like scroll inscriptions.
4. **Chorus 1 (61–86):** at 66.0 the silk tears down the middle, revealing the neon night street. The idol's face is
   outlined in neon tubes; neon tears drop on the beat. KHUÔN MẶT ĐÁNG THƯƠNG in neon.
5. **Verse 2 (86–121.6):** neon drips back into watercolour. A split screen: silk memory on the left, neon present on the
   right, drifting apart. The dense middle part is typeset in fast brushed fragments.
6. **Chorus 2 (121.6–146.8):** a bigger neon world: a rainy crossroads, the neon face on billboards, a porcelain mask
   cracking on the peak at 126.6.
7. **Breakdown (146.8–187.3):** "cánh hoa úa tàn". Back to silk, almost monochrome: one flower on a silk panel sheds
   petals one per phrase; petals fall into water and dissolve into rings. Very slow and quiet. From ~182 the handclaps
   start: ink dots spread on the beat.
8. **Final drop (187.3–227.7):** neon *over* silk: tubes drawn on top of the painting, the face in both media at once,
   everything pulsing. The biggest title.
9. **Outro (227.7–254.8):** the neon switches off tube by tube; the silk scroll rolls up, leaving one painted tear.
